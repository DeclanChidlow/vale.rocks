I love the CSS spec's take on supporting text-decoration: blink.

It may as well say,'Support it, don't support it, nobody here gives a shit. Probably don't make stuff blink though. We all know what a mess `<blink>` was.'

_(Even before deprecation, none of the major browsers supported the value.)_

<small>The relevant bit of CSS Text Decoration Module Level 4: [https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-decor-4/#text-decoration-line-property](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-decor-4/#text-decoration-line-property)</small>
