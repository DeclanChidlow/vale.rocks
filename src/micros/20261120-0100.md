I picked the TLD for this site without any great deal of thought -- certainly with less deliberation than I would now. The idea was 'Vale (me) rocks!'. A bit of a tongue-in-cheek ego play. Stumbling around the web, I found Vale.Rocks listed as one of the top websites using the `.rocks` TLD. Surely not, I thought. Vale.Rocks represents so much work and is my greatest creation, but it is niche.

Indeed, it is far from the biggest by measures of quantity of backlinks or traffic. However, [it is quite highly placed (position 94,257) in the Majestic Million](https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million?domain=vale.rocks), 'the million domains we find with the most referring subnets'. What does that mean? It means that instead of counting backlinks or traffic directly, it ranks based on how many subnets -- that is, ranges of IP addresses (specifically, C-class subnets) -- refer to a given site. Thus, the highest-ranking sites are those referred to by the widest _variety_ of places on the web because links from the same subnet are collapsed into a single vote.

[Filtering by the `.rocks` TLD](https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million?tld=rocks), it is presently in position 4 on the list, after `activitypub.rocks`, `kif.rocks`, and `koreader.rocks`. This indicates that while Vale.Rocks might not be the most popular site, it is one with a broad backlink profile, with links originating from a wide variety of networks from all over the web.
