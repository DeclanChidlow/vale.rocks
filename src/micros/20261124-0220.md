title: Ladybird Browser Politics
---

The Ladybird browser is an impressive feat, and, as of writing today, has [launched in Alpha](https://ladybird.org/posts/alpha/). I've been [rather vocal about the need for browser diversity](/posts/everything-is-chrome), but I can't help but feel melancholy about this release and all the joyous celebration I'm seeing from those seemingly unaware of the project's politics.

Ladybird is the project of Andreas Kling. He created the project, moved it out from SerenityOS, and serves as the president of the board. He has repeatedly demonstrated himself to be [a bad, hateful person](https://kvibber.com/reviews/software/ladybird-inclusivity/) who actively goes out of his way to be exclusionary. Drew DeVault's [_Weird little guys of FOSS_ entry on Kling](https://drewdevault.com/weird-guys/#kling) provides further details.

I can accept that people have differing views and politics, but I can't extend that to people who wish harm upon my friends because of their innate identities. As [supporting Harry Potter properties directly benefits JK Rowling](/micros/20261124-0020) and the horrid beliefs she holds and the pains she inflicts, by promoting or donating to Ladybird, you're rather directly supporting Andreas Kling without much dilution of responsibility.

I'm so glad to see more competition in the browser space, but I'm so saddened that the most complete browser engine outside of the big three is associated with such a vile person.
