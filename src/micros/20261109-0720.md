---
title: The Standard Galactic Alphabet
tags: [language]
---

Most famous for its use in Minecraft -- particularly in enchanting tables -- the Standard Galactic Alphabet originates from Commander Keen. It is not a separate language or anything of the sort. Instead, it is simply a substitution of Latin alphabet characters with bespoke symbols.

For the full and proper effect, you need a custom font, but here it is represented with Unicode symbols:

A sentence is ended with `._.`, but there are no other official punctuation or number symbols. Despite this, some people have taken it upon themselves to create these additional characters.
