The International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) uses binary (base-2), meaning steps of 1,024 ($2^{10}$) -- KiB, MiB, GiB, etc. Meanwhile, the International System of Units (SI) uses decimal (base-10), meaning steps of 1,000 ($10^3$) -- KB, MB, GB, etc.
