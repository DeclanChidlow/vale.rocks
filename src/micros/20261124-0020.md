title: Don't Buy Harry Potter Content
---

When you give money to corporate <abbr title="Intellectual Property">IP</abbr>, there is a certain dilution of responsibility. As the saying goes, 'There is no ethical consumption under capitalism'. Inherently some harm will be involved because companies operate in pursuit of profit, and because if you follow the money and deals, it'll always end up at least partially going somewhere you disagree with.

However, with an individual creator or rights holder (or a small group), this is substantially different. I can think of no other creative who has retained ownership of a major property, as JK Rowling has held on to Harry Potter. This is, plainly, quite impressive. Even other notable cases I can think of, like Bill Watterson with Calvin and Hobbes or Hergé with Tintin, are nowhere near the same scale. Unfortunately, JK Rowling is a hateful woman who dedicates her time, fame, and fortune to harming transgender people and seemingly very little else.

She considers any support of Harry Potter as support of herself and, by extension, her bigoted views. Buying Harry Potter content means money goes directly into her personal bank accounts. Harry Potter and JK Rowling are synonymous. You cannot separate them. Support Harry Potter? You're supporting harm against transgender people. Thankfully, it is uniquely easy not to support her: don't buy _Wizarding World_ content. In so many cases it is hard to boycott because it is hard to see the path money takes and it ends up in the hands of the wrong people eventually, but with JK Rowling the case is cut and dry.

If you follow the money going to HBO or Warner Bros Pictures, then that all goes to Skydance Corporation, which [has plenty of problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Skydance_Corporation) and which is presently led by the ghoul David Ellison (and his horribly foul father, Larry Ellison). It is so easy and obvious not to buy Harry Potter material. Get it secondhand if you must, and remember that piracy can be ethical.
