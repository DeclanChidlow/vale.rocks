---
title: Bending Spoons' Business Model
---

Bending Spoon is an Italian technology company. They acquire established companies with subscription-based products and high lock-in. They then aggressively cut any expenses possible by various means, including staff layoffs, while also reducing any liabilities. They then proceed to increase prices while dropping any non-profitable aspects of the company. Bending Spoons is a company that turns other companies into condemned profit extractors.

You can see this in companies they've acquired such as Evernote, Meetup, WeTransfer, Vimeo, AOL, and Eventbrite. They're husks, and the only updates they get are in pursuit of profit extraction -- not user benefit. Most users are disappointed and leave the platforms while a few remain and make the acquisition worth it. Over time Bending Spoons has been scaling up and buying larger and larger companies.

On an individual company level, this isn't a sustainable business model. It isn't one of growth. Nobody reasonable who isn't already locked in would adopt their services. They acquire companies, gut them, then hike up the prices, knowing that some people will stay due to the hassle of leaving. With no new incoming users, the existing users will slowly trickle out, and the product is dead in the water. Until then, however? Bending Spoons extracts as much value as possible.

They're purely leeches of the world. They destroy so much and create nothing but financial value for themselves and their shareholders.
