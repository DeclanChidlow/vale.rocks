Sitting on the balcony clutching a warm cup of tea against the cool wind as I look over ocean, my attention is captured by rabbits frolicking in the backyard. Hopping about and darting between their warrens.

They're cute, I feel, but also, they're terrible.

After being released into the wild for game hunting in the mid-1800s, rabbits have since -- as the saying goes -- bred like rabbits. Now there are a pervasive introduced species which compete with native fauna and cause massive damage to the environment.

They need to be killed en masse and removed from Australia. They have no place here and do so much harm.

But as I look at one groom the other by the fern, I know that these individual rabbits have no clue of their damage. They did not choose a fate of being here or being exterminated.

Humans played god and brought these living animals here as playthings to kill for fun. Now humans must play god again and kill them all due to long-past gross negligence. Cute little rabbits must be made dead little rabbits. Culled at human discretion because of decisions made by those who never lived to see the true consequences of their actions.

It isn't fair for the little rabbits who know no better. The group must die, but the individuals have done no wrong.
